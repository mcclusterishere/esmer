# WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT / RECORDING & PRODUCTION BARTER AGREEMENT

**Draft for review and signature — Connecticut**

This Website Development / Recording & Production Barter Agreement (the **“Agreement”**) is entered as of **________________, 2026** (the **“Effective Date”**) by and among:

**McCluster Corp**, a Connecticut public charity (CT Public Charity Registration CHR.0069693; ISNI 0000 0005 2872 7276), acting through Matthew McCluster, and **Matthew Christian Perry McCluster**, professionally known as Matthew McCluster (ISNI 0000 0005 2956 3111), individually as artist/songwriter where applicable (collectively, **“McCluster”**); and

**Justin Esmer**, professionally known as **“Esmer”** (**“Esmer”**).

McCluster and Esmer are each a **“Party”** and together the **“Parties.”**

## 1. Purpose and barter consideration

The Parties are exchanging professional services rather than paying one another a cash service fee.

1. McCluster will design, develop, prepare, and launch the Esmer website described in **Exhibit A** (the **“Website Services”**).
2. In exchange, Esmer will provide the studio access, recording, engineering, production, musical performance, and related services described in **Exhibit B** for McCluster's album currently titled **PRIM3** (the **“Production Services”**).
3. The Parties agree that these mutual promises and services are good and sufficient consideration for this Agreement. Neither Party owes the other an hourly service fee, producer fee, website-development fee, studio rental fee, or other cash service fee for the work expressly included in Exhibits A and B.
4. Any third-party hard cost — including a domain purchase, paid software/subscription, outside musician, equipment rental, travel outside the agreed local area, licensed sample, stock asset, or similar expense — requires advance written approval from the Party expected to pay it.
5. Each Party is responsible for its own tax, accounting, and reporting obligations arising from the barter.

## 2. Website Services

McCluster will provide the Website Services with Esmer's reasonable cooperation, timely delivery of assets, and timely approvals.

The Website will be a premium **mobile-first** artist/producer property with a persistent three-destination bottom navigation system:

- **Music** — authorized music playback and/or verified streaming links, releases, credits, videos, and recent work;
- **Esmer / Home** — an immersive, scroll-driven presentation of Esmer's identity, story, music, production work, live performance, press, and studio; and
- **Book** — a booking/inquiry path for the recording, production, engineering, musician, performance, collaboration, and other services Esmer actually approves for public offering.

The Website Services include the items specifically listed in Exhibit A. References to the McCluster website or features Esmer has seen on it describe the intended level of interaction, polish, and immersive presentation; they do not transfer McCluster's entire private platform, backend, control plane, unreleased assets, or unrelated business functions into the Esmer site.

### 2.1 Immersive / spatial studio experience

McCluster may photograph and film Esmer and the studio and may capture 360-degree, photogrammetric, spatial, or other source media. The Website will be architected to support an immersive studio experience, including a 3D/VR/spatial layer if the captured material and target devices reasonably support it. A performant conventional/mobile experience is the required foundation; 3D/VR is progressive enhancement and is not a promise that every device or browser will support WebXR.

Esmer authorizes McCluster to enter and capture the studio with Esmer's permission for the purpose of producing the Website and mutually approved promotional/case-study material. The Parties will not knowingly publish door/access codes, alarm information, exact private access routes, equipment serial numbers, confidential screens/documents, or other security-sensitive information.

### 2.2 Client assets and factual approvals

Esmer will provide or approve the artist name, biography, services, contact method, rates if any, social/streaming links, releases, artwork, photographs, logos/wordmarks, and any other factual or licensed content to be published. McCluster may use public research to prepare drafts, but Esmer is responsible for confirming personal facts and service claims before launch.

### 2.3 Revisions and post-launch correction period

The Website Services include **two reasonable consolidated revision rounds** after McCluster presents a substantially complete first build, plus **30 calendar days of correction of reproducible launch defects** after public launch. New features, major redesigns, new campaigns, ongoing content management, or maintenance after that period are outside this barter unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.

## 3. Production Services for PRIM3

Esmer will provide the Production Services reasonably necessary to complete the tracks identified in Exhibit B, subject to the agreed scope, scheduling, and creative direction.

Unless Exhibit B says otherwise, Production Services may include:

- reasonable access to Esmer's agreed studio/workspace for scheduled PRIM3 sessions;
- recording and session engineering;
- music production and arrangement assistance;
- Esmer's instrumental and/or vocal performance when requested and mutually agreed;
- editing and session preparation;
- collaboration with McCluster on sound and production decisions; and
- delivery of session materials as stated below.

### 3.1 Track list controls the exchange

Before signature or before the first material session under this Agreement, the Parties shall complete and initial **Exhibit B** with the PRIM3 track list and the Production Services expected for each track. The goal is completion of that identified album work, not an undefined lifetime entitlement to studio time.

### 3.2 Scheduling

The Parties will schedule sessions in good faith around school, work, performance, travel, and other professional commitments. Neither Party will unreasonably delay the exchange. Missed sessions should be rescheduled promptly.

### 3.3 Session-file delivery

For each completed or materially advanced PRIM3 track, Esmer will provide McCluster, upon request and no later than a reasonable period after the applicable session, the materials in Esmer's possession that are necessary for McCluster to preserve and continue the project, including where applicable:

- raw recorded multitracks at the native session sample rate/bit depth;
- consolidated stems or grouped exports;
- current reference bounce;
- relevant session/project files where legally and technically transferable; and
- a list of any third-party plug-ins, samples, performers, or licensed material required to reproduce the session.

No deliverable may intentionally include pirated software, unlicensed commercial samples, or material Esmer has no authority to provide.

## 4. PRIM3 master ownership; work-made-for-hire and assignment

The Parties intend that **McCluster will exclusively own the PRIM3 master recordings and the production contributions commissioned under this Agreement**, except only as a later signed writing expressly states otherwise.

### 4.1 Work made for hire to the extent legally available

To the maximum extent permitted by United States copyright law, each copyrightable sound-recording contribution, production element, edit, recording, performance fixed in a master, and other copyrightable material created by Esmer specifically for PRIM3 under this Agreement is specially commissioned by McCluster and shall be deemed a **work made for hire** for McCluster where the legal requirements for that status are satisfied, including where an applicable contribution qualifies as a contribution to a collective work or compilation.

### 4.2 Present backup assignment

Because independent-contractor sound-recording work does not automatically qualify as work made for hire in every circumstance, **Esmer hereby presently assigns, transfers, and conveys to McCluster Corp all right, title, and interest that Esmer owns or later acquires in and to the PRIM3 master-recording contributions created under this Agreement**, including all copyrights and exclusive rights in those contributions, throughout the world for the full copyright term and any renewals or extensions, subject only to rights that applicable law does not permit a person to waive or transfer.

This assignment includes, to the extent Esmer owns them, rights in recorded performances, production elements, session edits, sound design, engineering authorship, arrangement embodied only in the sound recording, and other sound-recording authorship created for PRIM3.

Esmer will sign reasonable further documents necessary to confirm the ownership granted here, provided those documents do not materially expand the economic bargain beyond this Agreement.

### 4.3 No master share, producer points, or backend unless separately signed

The barter consideration is the compensation for Esmer's Production Services under this Agreement. Esmer receives **no ownership percentage of a PRIM3 master, producer points, royalty, backend participation, distribution share, neighboring-right ownership claim, or other continuing master participation** unless a later song-specific or project-specific written addendum is signed by both Parties and expressly grants it.

A courtesy or contractual production/engineering/performance credit does not create ownership or royalties.

## 5. Musical compositions, songwriting, and publishing

McCluster's pre-existing lyrics, melodies, musical compositions, concepts, and other composition material remain McCluster's property. Production, engineering, mixing decisions, playing an instrument from McCluster's direction, or ordinary arrangement assistance do not by themselves create an agreed songwriting or publishing split between the Parties.

### 5.1 Split sheet required for any retained writing share

Esmer will not intentionally add original lyrics, melody, or other protectable musical-composition material to a final PRIM3 song with the expectation of retaining a writer/publisher share unless the Parties first or contemporaneously sign a **song-specific split sheet** identifying the song, writers, percentages, and publishing treatment.

If Esmer creates protectable musical-composition material specifically for PRIM3, that material is used in a PRIM3 release, and **no contrary song-specific split sheet is signed**, then for the barter consideration Esmer hereby assigns to McCluster Corp all of Esmer's transferable right, title, and interest in that PRIM3-specific composition contribution for the full copyright term, subject only to nonwaivable law.

No writer share, publisher share, PRO registration, mechanical interest, performance income share, or synchronization income share is owed to Esmer unless established by a separate signed split sheet or addendum.

## 6. McCluster control of release, registration, and metadata

McCluster has final authority over the PRIM3 album's creative release decisions, master selection, title, sequencing, artwork, distribution, copyright registration, rights administration, publishing administration, ISRC assignment, DDEX metadata/delivery, distributor submissions, licensing, takedowns, edits, remixes, derivatives, and exploitation, subject to any later signed third-party agreement.

Nothing in this Agreement authorizes Esmer to distribute, leak, sell, upload, register, claim, Content-ID, monetize, or otherwise exploit unreleased PRIM3 masters, stems, compositions, or session materials on Esmer's own behalf.

## 7. Third-party musicians, samples, and material

Esmer shall not knowingly introduce into a PRIM3 master any third-party performer, co-producer, engineer, sample, beat, loop, composition, recording, AI-generated asset subject to restrictive terms, or other rights-bearing material without disclosure to McCluster and, where material, McCluster's prior written approval.

If Esmer brings an outside contributor into a PRIM3 recording, the contributor must sign a release/work-for-hire/assignment or other rights document reasonably acceptable to McCluster before the contribution is treated as final. McCluster may reject or replace a contribution that arrives with unclear rights.

Esmer represents that material Esmer supplies as original is not knowingly copied from or encumbered by a third party and that Esmer will disclose known license restrictions.

## 8. Credits and portfolio use

### 8.1 Esmer credit on PRIM3

McCluster will use commercially reasonable efforts to credit Esmer accurately for the material services Esmer actually performs, such as producer, recording engineer, musician, studio, or other role, subject to final contribution, metadata-field availability, and any mutually approved credit language. Credit does not alter Sections 4 or 5.

### 8.2 PRIM3 as Esmer recent work

After the applicable PRIM3 material is publicly released, McCluster grants Esmer permission to identify the released work as a portfolio/recent-work example using factually accurate approved credit language, public artwork or approved imagery, and links/embeds from authorized public sources. The Esmer site should link back to the canonical McCluster/PRIM3 destination supplied by McCluster.

Esmer may not publish unreleased PRIM3 audio, stems, lyrics, artwork, story material, session screens, or confidential information merely for portfolio purposes.

### 8.3 McCluster portfolio use of Esmer Website

Esmer grants McCluster a perpetual, worldwide, nonexclusive, royalty-free license to display the publicly launched Website, screenshots, screen recordings, approved studio/site imagery, Esmer's professional name, and approved project description solely as a McCluster portfolio/case-study example and to identify McCluster as the designer/developer, without falsely implying endorsement of unrelated products.

## 9. Website intellectual property

### 9.1 Esmer materials

Esmer retains ownership of Esmer's pre-existing music, recordings, compositions, trademarks, stage name rights, logos, artwork, photographs/video Esmer owns, biographies, and other materials Esmer supplies to McCluster, subject to any third-party rights. Esmer grants McCluster the license needed to reproduce, adapt for display, host, and publish those materials as part of the Website and agreed portfolio display.

### 9.2 McCluster background technology

McCluster retains all ownership of its pre-existing and reusable code, components, libraries, utilities, workflows, methods, design systems, control-plane infrastructure, backend services, deployment tooling, know-how, templates, and other technology or intellectual property that existed before this project or is reasonably reusable across projects (**“McCluster Background Technology”**).

### 9.3 Site-specific deliverables after completion of barter

After Esmer has materially completed the Production Services required by Exhibit B, McCluster assigns to Esmer the copyright McCluster owns in the **custom front-end code and original site-specific copy/design elements created uniquely for the Esmer Website**, excluding McCluster Background Technology, third-party/open-source components, fonts, platform services, and any content owned by others.

To the extent the functioning Website contains McCluster Background Technology that cannot reasonably be separated, McCluster grants Esmer a perpetual, worldwide, nonexclusive, royalty-free license to use that embedded Background Technology solely as necessary to operate, maintain, and display the Esmer Website and reasonable successor versions of that site. This does not grant Esmer ownership of the McCluster control plane or the right to resell McCluster platform infrastructure as a standalone product.

### 9.4 Powered by McCluster attribution

The public Website will carry a visible but unobtrusive **“Powered by McCluster”** attribution linked to the canonical McCluster site. Maintaining that attribution is a material term of this barter unless Matthew McCluster later approves its removal in writing.

## 10. Photography, video, likeness, and studio-property permission

McCluster owns the copyright in original photographs, video, spatial captures, 3D captures/models, and similar media McCluster personally creates during the Esmer project, except to the extent a tool/provider's lawful terms provide otherwise.

Esmer grants McCluster permission to capture and use Esmer's name, professional identity, approved likeness, voice, performance, and studio property appearance for the Website and the limited portfolio uses in Section 8.3. McCluster grants Esmer a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license to use the final approved McCluster-created capture media as incorporated in or delivered for the Esmer Website and Esmer's own promotion, excluding raw or private material that the Parties mark confidential.

Neither Party may use the other's likeness/name to imply sponsorship of unrelated products without permission.

## 11. Confidentiality and unreleased work

Each Party shall use reasonable care to protect the other's nonpublic project files, unreleased music, credentials, private contact information, session materials, business information, and security-sensitive studio information. Disclosure to a contractor is permitted only when reasonably necessary for the project and subject to an appropriate duty of confidentiality.

Embargoed PRIM3 materials remain confidential until McCluster makes them public or gives written permission for a specific disclosure.

## 12. Creative control and approvals

- **PRIM3:** McCluster has final creative and release approval.
- **Esmer Website:** Esmer has final approval over factual biography, Esmer-owned brand materials, public services/rates, and the public portrayal of Esmer, while McCluster retains reasonable control over implementation quality, code integrity, and protection of McCluster Background Technology.
- Neither Party may demand that the other make a false factual statement, use unlicensed material, violate law, expose credentials/security information, or misstate a music credit.

## 13. Independent contractors; no partnership

The Parties are independent contractors. This barter does not create a partnership, joint venture, employment relationship, record label agreement, management agreement, agency, or fiduciary relationship. Neither Party may bind the other to third-party obligations without written authority.

## 14. Representations and authority

Each Party represents that:

1. the Party has authority to enter this Agreement;
2. the Party has the rights necessary to provide materials the Party supplies for use under this Agreement, or will disclose limitations before use;
3. the Party will not knowingly require the other to infringe third-party rights; and
4. the Party will perform its obligations in good faith.

## 15. Delays, breach, cure, and termination

If a Party materially breaches this Agreement, the non-breaching Party will provide written notice describing the breach and allow **10 business days** to cure when the breach is reasonably curable.

If the breach is not cured, the non-breaching Party may suspend further performance and terminate the unperformed portion of the Agreement. Termination does not undo rights in PRIM3 material already created and assigned under Sections 4 and 5, nor confidentiality obligations, accrued payment/hard-cost obligations, or licenses/ownership that by their nature should survive.

If Esmer materially abandons the Production Services before satisfying Exhibit B, McCluster is not required to transfer ownership of the site-specific deliverables under Section 9.3, although the Parties may sign a written partial-completion settlement. If McCluster materially abandons the Website Services after Esmer has substantially performed the Production Services, Esmer retains all other remedies available under this Agreement and applicable law.

## 16. No guarantee of commercial results

Neither Party guarantees streams, followers, bookings, revenue, chart placement, label interest, awards, search ranking, press coverage, or other commercial result. The obligations are professional services and deliverables, not guaranteed market outcomes.

## 17. Governing law and disputes

Connecticut law governs this Agreement without regard to conflict-of-law principles. Before filing suit, the Parties will first attempt in good faith to resolve a dispute directly. If unresolved, either Party may pursue available remedies in a court of competent jurisdiction in Connecticut. Nothing prevents a Party from seeking prompt injunctive relief when reasonably necessary to prevent unauthorized release, infringement, misuse of confidential material, or comparable irreparable harm.

## 18. Entire agreement; amendments; electronic signatures

This Agreement and its completed exhibits are the entire agreement concerning this barter and supersede prior oral or text-message descriptions of the exchange. Any material amendment, royalty, ownership change, songwriting split, new cash fee, scope expansion, or removal of the Powered by McCluster requirement must be in a writing signed by both Parties.

Electronic signatures and counterparts are effective as originals. A signed PDF or reputable e-signature record may be used as evidence of execution.
