## 2. Artist-name origin and creative background

### VERIFIED PUBLIC, BUT PERSONAL — CLIENT APPROVAL REQUIRED BEFORE PROMINENT PUBLIC USE

In a long-form interview with Your Favorite Groupies, Esmer said the project name is a homage to his father. He described receiving his first recording equipment from his father around 2018–2019 and choosing **Esmer**, the family surname, as the project identity.

The same interview says:

- He began with piano and cello lessons.
- He picked up guitar in church around fourth grade.
- He later taught himself bass and drums in high school.
- He wrote poetry in middle school and gradually converted writing into songs and raps.
- He played in a high-school cover band, **Bad Habits**.
- During his first semester at Southern Connecticut, he filled in with a frat band called **Coi**.
- A 2022 Milford show helped make a professional music path feel tangible to him.
- He described live performance and meeting people through music as a central part of why he continues the work.

The Daily Campus independently reports that he began songwriting in middle school and took it much more seriously during the pandemic period in high school. It reports his first single, **Peace of Mind**, was released at the end of 2020.

## 3. Musical identity

### VERIFIED PUBLIC

Commonly reported descriptors:

- Alternative
- R&B / soul
- Jazz influence
- Alternative rock
- Hip-hop influence in some earlier venue bios
- Smooth / baritone vocal presentation
- Guitar-led songwriting
- Strong use of harmony
- Introspective lyric writing

The Daily Campus reported that Esmer often starts songs from a quick vocal melody or guitar chord progression, records ideas into voice memos, and then decides what emotional or sonic "vibe" fits the melody.

Earlier in his recording career he frequently recorded the instruments, produced, and mixed his music himself. Public interviews describe a later shift toward more collaboration with other musicians and producers.

## 5. Achilles Heel creative-process notes

### VERIFIED PUBLIC, CLIENT APPROVAL REQUIRED FOR PERSONAL STORY USE

The Your Favorite Groupies interview provides unusually useful source material for the site's **Logs / Process / Selected Work** layer:

- Esmer described Achilles Heel as a deliberate move toward greater collaboration.
- He credited friends/collaborators across different tracks, including Cam, Izzy, Khi, Mike, Luca, Adam/Schrubbe, Darian, Claire, Ben, and Joe.
- He described recording material while spending time in Los Angeles and meeting collaborators at Occidental College.
- He described `Waves` as vocally driven and emotionally exposed, with a minimal arrangement creating space for the vocal.
- He described `Ponyo` and `Waves` as conceptually related, with ocean/beach imagery functioning as refuge and progress toward a dream.
- He said collaboration helped him loosen earlier control over every part of the recording process.
- He described `2nd Street` as involving collaborative guitar-tone development and `Achilles Heel` as pursuing a large, emotionally expressive guitar moment.

Cafeteria Media likewise describes Achilles Heel as Esmer's third EP, built around vulnerability, introspection, guitar, storytelling, collaboration, and a community-centered release event.

These notes are excellent candidates for the editorial/log treatment Justin requested. Use paraphrase, original interview excerpts only when licensed/within quotation limits, and preferably obtain Esmer's direct approved wording during the content pass.
